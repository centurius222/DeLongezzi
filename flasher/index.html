DeLongezzi Flasher

Installs DeLongezzi v2.2.3 on an ESP32-S3 N16R8 board, straight from the browser.

Before you start

YOUR WI-FI MUST BE 2.4 GHZ: THE ESP32-S3 BOARD CAN'T CONNECT TO 5 GHZ.

  • This erases everything on the board (settings, shots, profiles, themes, videos) and installs the firmware with 32 themes, 18 brew-button videos and pictures, and 5 starter profiles.
  • Only for an ESP32-S3 N16R8 board.
This browser can't flash a board over USB. Use Chrome on a desktop or laptop. Flashing only works from an https:// page or from localhost.

Plug the board in by USB, press the button and pick its port (usually "USB JTAG/serial debug unit"). Flashing the 16 MB file takes a few minutes.

After flashing
  1. If the flasher asks for your Wi-Fi, enter it there.
  2. Otherwise join the board's hotspot DeLongezzi (password delongezzi), open http://4.4.4.1, enter your Wi-Fi name and password and press Save & restart.
  3. The board restarts into your Wi-Fi and is then at http://delongezzi.local. If that doesn't open, find its address in your router's device list. Change the hotspot password there: the default is public.
  4. The web page has no login: keep the machine on your home network.
  5. Set the water levels under Settings › Calibration: empty the tank and press Set Empty, then fill it and press Set Full.
