# MAX31865 for the PT1000A probe

[← Back to the parts list](BOM.md)

This build uses a PT1000A probe on 3 wires, so the MAX31865 module needs two changes before you
wire it. Use a fine soldering tip: the resistor is tiny (0805).

<img src="max31865-board.jpg" alt="MAX31865 board: 1 swap Rref for 4.3 kΩ, 2 solder-bridge 2/3 Wire, 3 cut the trace left of the middle pad, 4 solder-bridge the middle pad to 3, 5 probe: red pair in RTD+ and F+, blue in RTD-">

## 1. Reference resistor (PT1000)
Remove the factory reference resistor next to "Rref" and solder the 4.3 kΩ 0.1 % one in its place.

## 2. 3-wire mode
1. Solder-bridge the "2/3 Wire" pads.
2. Cut the thin trace between the middle pad and "24".
3. Solder-bridge the middle pad to "3".

## 3. Connect the probe
1. With a multimeter, find the two probe wires that read under 2 Ω between them (red in the picture).
   The third wire (blue) reads about 1100 Ω to them: that is the PT1000 itself.
2. Put the pair in RTD+ and F+, either way round.
3. Put the third wire in RTD-.
