# Which DeLonghi?

[← Back to the README](../README.md)

DeLongezzi was built and tested on a **DeLonghi ECP3220**.

## The same machine inside
Every ECP model has the same 150 ml stainless steel boiler, aluminum brew group, 51 mm portafilter
and body shape. Only the finish and the steam wand differ, so the cheapest one does the job just as well.

| Model | Sold in | Body | Steam wand |
|---|---|---|---|
| ECP3120 | US, Canada | Mostly plastic | Basic |
| ECP3220 | US, Canada | Black, white or red | Basic |
| ECP3420 | US, Canada | Metal front, cup warmer and drip tray cover | Basic |
| ECP3620 | US, Canada | Like the ECP3420, plus a metal housing | Basic |
| ECP3630 | US, Canada | Like the ECP3420, plus a metal housing | With an air switch (hot milk or cappuccino) |
| ECP31.21 | Europe, rest of world | Like the ECP3120 | Basic |
| ECP35.31 | Europe, rest of world | Like the ECP3420 | Basic |

## US or Europe
- **US and Canada:** the knob turns the machine on and it stays on. The cord has a 2-prong plug, so
  the boiler isn't grounded; the [parts list](BOM.md) has a 3-prong cord for that.
- **Europe and the rest of the world:** a push button turns it on, and it switches itself off after
  10 minutes idle. The GaggiMate community guide replaces the switch to stop that. Not tested with DeLongezzi.

## Stilosa
Every Stilosa is the same machine inside: one DeLonghi data sheet covers the EC230, EC235, EC260
and EC265 (stainless steel boiler, 51 mm portafilter, 15 bar pump, 1000 W heater, same wiring).
They differ only in baskets and steam wand. The GaggiMate community guide covers the Stilosa.

| Model | Sold in | Baskets | Steam wand |
|---|---|---|---|
| EC230 | Europe, rest of world | Non-pressurized | Plain tube |
| EC235 | Europe, rest of world | Pressurized, takes ESE pods | Plain tube |
| EC260 | Everywhere (EC260BK in the US) | Pressurized | Frothing sleeve (panarello) |

## Other EC models
- **EC702:** preferred by the community: it's bigger, so there's more room inside for the parts.
  The GaggiMate community guide names it as compatible.
- **Other EC models** (like the EC155 or the Dedica line): not tested.
